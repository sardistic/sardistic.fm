Where this comes from. Your scrobble database on the Toughbook (/srv/sardistic.fm/data/analytics.db), read directly — every play count, year range and top album here is your own listening history, not an editorial guess.

Why some stores open in a tab. Seven of the nine refuse to be embedded in another page: eBay, Depop, Mercari, Grailed and Goodwill send X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, ThredUp sends DENY, and Poshmark's frame-ancestors list excludes us. Only Etsy and Vinted allow it, so those render inline. The rest reuse a single named tab rather than piling up nine.

What was filtered out. The raw top 300 is not a merch list, so 39 entries were dropped: record labels, score composers, League of Legends cinematic titles, collab credits whose lead act already appears alone, a literal https, unicode garbage, and sardistic — your own uploads. 45 artists carry a search override because the bare name searches badly.
